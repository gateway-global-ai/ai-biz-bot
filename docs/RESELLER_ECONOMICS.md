# Reseller Program Economics

Reference numbers for the Reseller & Affiliate program (Clear Voice AI / AI Biz Bot). Use for sales, affiliate materials, and pipeline planning.

## Assumptions

- **Average client revenue (platform):** $169/month
- **Customer lifetime:** 14 months
- **LTV per client (platform):** $169 × 14 = **$2,366**

Commission tiers (by number of businesses): Bronze 8%, Silver 10%, Gold 12%, Platinum 14%, Diamond 16%.

## Commission per client by tier

| Tier     | Commission | Per client/month (reseller) | Per client LTV (14 months) |
|:---------|:----------:|----------------------------:|---------------------------:|
| Bronze   | 8%         | $13.52                      | $189.28                    |
| Silver   | 10%        | $16.90                      | $236.60                    |
| Gold     | 12%        | $20.28                      | $283.92                    |
| Platinum | 14%        | $23.66                      | $331.24                    |
| Diamond  | 16%        | $27.04                      | $378.56                    |

Average recurring revenue to the reseller is the per-client/month value above, depending on tier.

## Example: one big client (100 locations)

- **Platform revenue:** 100 × $169/month = $16,900/month
- **Reseller tier for 100 businesses:** Gold (51–100)
- **Commission rate:** 12%
- **Reseller monthly commission:** $16,900 × 12% = **$2,028/month**
- **LTV (14 months):** $2,028 × 14 = **$28,392**

If the reseller is already at Platinum (101–500 total businesses), the same 100-location client earns 14%:

- **Reseller monthly commission:** $16,900 × 14% = **$2,366/month**
- **LTV (14 months):** $2,366 × 14 = **$33,124**
