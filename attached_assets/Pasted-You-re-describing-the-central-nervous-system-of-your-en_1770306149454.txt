Here is the architecture and documentation for this core feature.

### **Core Concept: The AI Bot Matrix**
Every page in the customer's site or dashboard becomes a potential **host for one or more specialized AI agents**. You control a three-dimensional matrix:
1.  **Bot:** The specialized AI persona (Sales, Accounting, Onboarding, etc.).
2.  **Admin Controls:** The tools, models, and prompts *you* configure for that bot.
3.  **User Controls:** How the end-user (the site visitor) interacts with it (Chat, Voice, Form).

### **Implementation Blueprint**

#### **1. The Universal Admin Menu**
A floating or sidebar menu (like in your screenshot) available on every page in "edit" mode. It contains:
*   **Page Bots:** List of bots active on this page (`+ Add Bot`).
*   **Bot Library:** Gallery of pre-built bots.
*   **AI Bot Wizard:** Create a custom bot.

#### **2. The Pre-Built Bot Library**
Pre-configured, one-click deploy agents:

| Bot | Core Purpose | Default System Prompt Vibe | Best For Page |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sales Bot** | Qualify leads, answer product questions, schedule demos. | "You are a helpful, low-pressure sales assistant. Focus on understanding needs, not pushing sales." | Product, Pricing. |
| **Onboarding Bot** | Guide new users/customers through setup. | "You are a patient teacher. Guide users step-by-step. Never assume they know terminology." | Dashboard, Welcome. |
| **Support Bot** | Troubleshoot issues, find docs, escalate tickets. | "You are a pragmatic problem-solver. Acknowledge frustration, focus on solutions." | Help, Contact. |
| **Coding Bot** (Dev Tier) | Explain code, generate snippets, debug. | "You are a pair programmer. Explain concepts, write secure, commented code." | Documentation, API. |

#### **3. The AI Bot Wizard (For Custom Bots)**
A step-by-step UI that uses your own AI to generate the perfect system prompt and configuration.
1.  **Name Your Bot:** "VIP Customer Upsell Bot"
2.  **Select its Primary Mode:** `Sales`, `Support`, `Creative`, `Analytical`, `Coding`.
3.  **Define its Goal:** "Its main job is to identify customers who bought X and offer them premium add-on Y."
4.  **Wizard Generates:**
    *   A tailored **system prompt**.
    *   Recommended **model** (Kimi for long context, Claude for safety, GPT-4 for reasoning).
    *   Suggested **tools** to enable (e.g., access to the customer's Shopify order API).
    *   Default **UI** (minimal chat button, full-page takeover, voice-only).

#### **4. Page-Level Bot Configuration Panel**
Clicking a bot in the menu opens its fine-grained controls:
*   **Identity:** Name, avatar, short description (visible to user).
*   **Brain:** System prompt editor (with version history), model selector (Kimi, Claude, GPT), temperature.
*   **Tools & Knowledge:** Toggle access to:
    *   **Page Context:** Can read the page's text/content.
    *   **Business Data:** Access to Google Places reviews, operating hours.
    *   **Integrations:** Connect to this business's specific Square/Shopify API.
    *   **Custom Docs:** Upload a PDF manual for it to reference.
*   **Interaction & UI:**
    *   **Default Interface:** Chat, Voice, or Both.
    *   **Opening Message:** Customizable first message.
    *   **Placement:** Floating button, embedded widget, top bar.

#### **5. The "WordPress" Backend: Database Schema**
