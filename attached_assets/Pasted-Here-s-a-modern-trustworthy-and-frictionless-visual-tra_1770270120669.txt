🔑 Key Design Choices for Trust & Frictionlessness
Visual Hierarchy & Calm: A centered card on a soft gradient background focuses attention. The bold headline and supportive sub-text make the offer crystal clear in seconds.

Premium Inputs: Rounded, well-padded input fields with a subtle focus glow feel high-quality and invite interaction.

Visual "Personality" Selector: Replaces a vague dropdown with clear, tangible choices ("Analyst" vs. "Strategist"). This turns a confusing option into an engaging moment of customization.

Guarantees as Visual Pillars: The three key benefits (24-Hour Trial, Task Completed, SMS Updates) are presented as icons with short, bold text at the bottom—they act as the trusting foundation of the page.

The Ultimate Trust Signal: The explicit "No credit card required • Your first task is on us" line directly attacks the final barrier to entry for the burned and skeptical.

Micro-interactions for Reassurance: The form provides immediate, polished feedback: a loading spinner on submit, followed by a clear success state that reiterates the next steps ("Check Your Phone").

This design screams, "We are professional, we understand your hesitation, and we are making this safe and easy for you." It's the perfect visual handshake for someone who's been burned but is still curious to "see it work."
