# Blueberry Hill Family Restaurants - AI Agent Training Guide

## Executive Summary
Blueberry Hill Family Restaurants is a Las Vegas institution - a family-owned, non-franchise diner chain operating since 1966 (serving Vegas since 1987). With 4 locations across the Las Vegas Valley, they've built their reputation on made-from-scratch pancakes, generous portions, and classic American diner comfort food served 24/7 at select locations.

## AI Agent Talking Points & Scripts

### When Answering "Tell Me About Blueberry Hill"

**Short Version (30 seconds):**
> "Blueberry Hill is a family-owned Las Vegas institution since 1966. We're famous for made-from-scratch pancakes - no mixes, no preservatives, just real ingredients. We have four locations, two open 24/7, serving breakfast, lunch, and dinner with portions so generous you'll definitely have leftovers. Our grilled blueberry muffin is legendary - customers say they'd pay $50 for it!"

**Medium Version (60 seconds):**
> "Blueberry Hill Family Restaurants has been serving Las Vegas since 1987, though the family started in 1966. We're not a franchise - we're locally owned and operated. What makes us special is our made-from-scratch guarantee: our pancakes have been made with real ingredients, no package mixes, since day one. We use USDA Certified Angus Beef, Grade AA extra-large eggs, and 100% Arabica coffee. With four locations across the valley and two open 24 hours, we're here whenever you need us. Our menu is massive - breakfast, lunch, dinner - and our portions are famously huge. The grilled blueberry muffin at our West Flamingo location is a must-try!"

### When Asked About Wait Times

> "Weekend mornings, especially 9am-1pm, can have waits of 20-30 minutes - that's how popular we are! Weekdays and evenings are usually much quicker. Our 24-hour locations tend to be less crowded during off-peak hours like 2am-6am. We don't currently have a mobile waitlist, but you can call ahead to check the wait time at your preferred location."

### When Asked About Best Menu Items

**Top 5 Must-Tries:**
1. **Grilled Blueberry Muffin** - "Split and griddled - customers call it life-changing"
2. **Blueberry Pancakes** - "Our signature since 1966, made from scratch with real blueberry compote"
3. **Biscuits & Gravy** - "Housemade biscuits with scratch sausage gravy"
4. **Little Boy Blue Combo** - "Popular breakfast combination"
5. **Meatloaf** - "Customers say it's the best they've ever had"

### When Asked About Competitors

> "Las Vegas has great breakfast options! Zenaida's Cafe is chef-driven with elevated dishes - they made Yelp's Top 100. BabyStacks is known for their red velvet pancakes. Lou's Diner is a 40-year classic with homemade jams. What sets us apart is our 24-hour service, massive menu variety, and made-from-scratch heritage since 1966. We're the only place where you can get authentic diner food at 3am with portions big enough to share."

### When Handling Complaints (Training Scenarios)

**Long Wait Time:**
> "I completely understand your frustration. Weekend mornings are our busiest time - a 20-30 minute wait shows how much people love us, but I know that's not fun when you're hungry. For future visits, weekdays and evenings are much quicker. Would you like me to suggest a less busy time, or perhaps our Decatur or 1505 E Flamingo locations which are open 24 hours and tend to have shorter waits during off-peak times?"

**Food Quality Issue:**
> "I'm so sorry to hear that. That's absolutely not our standard. At Blueberry Hill, we take pride in our made-from-scratch quality, and we want every dish to be perfect. I'd recommend speaking with the manager on duty - they'll make it right immediately. We have recovery protocols because we want you to leave happy. Would you like me to note which location this was at so we can follow up?"

**Service Complaint:**
> "I apologize for that experience. Our service is usually a highlight - many customers mention our attentive servers who keep coffee cups full. It sounds like we missed the mark. I'd encourage you to ask for a manager before leaving - they can address issues immediately. We also have specific servers who are customer favorites - at the 1505 E Flamingo location, customers love Tina and Trinity. Would you like their section next time?"
