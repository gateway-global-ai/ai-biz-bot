ok, This is going to get real easy now. I met with the owner of BlueBerry Hill restaurants and he is the iconic example of who has not implemented anything AI. He is a friend of mine and I had not seen him for awhile and I met with him, showed him a demo of the current app from aistudio and he actually was impressed and had a couple questions.  As the app stands, it can be sold to even the most difficult of owners who does not trust AI at all. Here is what I found out. 

1. Loves the simplicity.
2. Hates tech so much that they cut off all the delivery services.
3. 90% of phone calls are non revenue producing.
4. Magic moment was entering business name, creating a web site significantly better than what he has currently, and demonstrating an AI agent who knew his business in 60 seconds.
5. Likes the thought of having AI answer all the phone calls.
6. Likes the ability to SMS the agent and find out what is going on with the website, not so interested in the admin panel. 
7. Manager selling point was reduction of incoming phone calls through screening to free up resources. Spanish recognition would be a plus as well as spanish translated website.
