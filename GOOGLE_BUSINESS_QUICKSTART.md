## ❓ Common Questions

### "What is Google Places?"
**Simple answer:** It's Google's database of businesses. When someone searches for "pizza near me," Google Places is what provides the results.

**For your business:** We use it to automatically fill in your business information so you don't have to type everything manually.

### "What is AI Content Generation?"
**Simple answer:** AI reads about your business and writes professional website content.

**How it helps:** Instead of hiring a copywriter for $500+, AI writes your website content in seconds based on your Google reviews and business type.

### "What is Voice AI?"
**Simple answer:** It's like having an employee who answers phone calls, but on your website instead.

**How it helps:** Customers visiting your website can ask questions out loud. The AI responds using information about your business. It's like a 24/7 receptionist that never takes a break.

### "What is Google Workspace?"
**Simple answer:** It's the business version of Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Docs.

**How it helps:**
- **Instead of:** name@gmail.com → **You get:** name@yourbusiness.com
- **Instead of:** Personal calendar → **You get:** Appointment booking system
- **Instead of:** Emailing Word files → **You get:** Professional documents that customers can view online

### "Do I need Google Workspace?"
**Basic Website:** No, your website works without it.

**Professional Business:** Yes, if you want:
- Professional email address
- Online appointment booking
- Document creation for invoices/proposals
- Team collaboration tools

**Cost comparison:**
- Hiring receptionist: $15/hour × 40 hours = $600/week
- Google Workspace: $6-12/month
- Our integration: $99 one-time

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "I can't find my business"
**Solution:**
1. Make sure you have a Google Business Profile
2. Try searching with your address instead of name
3. Check if business name spelling matches Google exactly

**Still stuck?** Contact support - we'll add it manually.

### "The AI content isn't accurate"
**Solution:**
1. Click "Edit" on any section
2. Change the text to what you want
3. Click "Save"

**The AI learns:** The more you edit, the better it gets at your business type.

### "Voice AI doesn't understand questions"
**Solution:**
1. Make sure you've filled in complete business information
2. Add your hours, services, and description
3. Train it by testing common questions

**It improves:** Voice AI gets smarter as customers use it.

### "Google Workspace connection failed"
**Solution:**
1. Make sure you're using a Google Workspace account (not personal Gmail)
2. Check that you're the account admin
3. Try disconnecting and reconnecting

**Alternative:** Contact support for manual setup assistance.
